# War

It's useful to define 'war' in a specific way that identifies 'optimal conflicts'.


## Optimal definitions

'War' is optimally defined as a 'solution to ambiguous conflicts'.


## Optimal conflicts

'Optimal wars' involve solving the problems of:
- 'resource distribution' in general (or specifically, personal/group rights distribution/definition/interactions)
- solving concept conflicts (like 'priority conflicts' and identifying new useful priorities)
- optimizations (solving for 'optimization' in general can also solve specific problems like 'avoiding war')
- prediction (predict the outcome of an ambiguous conflict, like by 'adding certainty structures' to 'clearly differentiate the ambiguous alternatives', or 'identify the net probability of one side winning in all possible/adjacent cases')
- conflict resolution: identifying/filtering alternates (identify the solution to all 'comparisons of ambiguous/similar alternatives')
- identifying superior conflicts to fight/resolve than the current conflict

So for example, 'wars over land' can be fought/resolved by 'defining property (or property rights) an optimal way', like 'whoever understands/applies the property the optimal way for the optimal intents'.
