# Relevant graph intents

It's useful to identify what structures are (and should be) 'problem/solution structures' on a graph, and 'which problems to solve', and what problems to solve 'in what structure'.


## Examples

Examples of graphs with well-defined problem/solution structures:
- problem/solution graphs have a default problems/intents like 'differentiating problems/solutions' or 'connecting problems (or problem variation/structures) with matching solutions (or solution variation/structures)'.

These graphs also have 'general graph intents' to optimize for:
- additionally trivializing connections that should be trivial

These graphs also have other problem/solution structures:
- problem/solution structures in 'graph variants/alternatives'
- problem/solution structures in the 'graph lifecycle' (from definition to generation/implementation to optimization)
- problem/solution structures in 'related graph sequences/graphs'
- problem/solution structures 'emerging in the same graph' (like 'trivialing some subset of prioritized connections creates suboptimal differences')


## Other intents related to graph intents

A relevant problem sequence is: 
- what graph structures should be applied as problem/solution structures, should those problems be solved, should those solutions be optimized, should those problems/solutions be applied as constants/variables, should another graph be generated to solve emergent problems, what general/optimal graph intents should be solved for, what related graphs should be generated to optimize the original graph, should alternate graphs be optimized in parallel, etc

The problem to solve is: 
- 'if the concept of "problem" is defined generally as a "difference to connect" (where a 'solution that is a similarity' still requires a 'difference in state'), should problems apply difference in every way and what structures should be different' (like 'should all problems be structured differently from other problems' and 'should problems always be represented as different from solutions' and 'should graphs of problems be as different as possible from graphs of solutions')
- as in, "to what degree/in what way should a 'graph of the definition of a problem or optimality' be 'integrated into other graphs'"
